Twenty-seven years of building good schools, not just good results.

Greenwood Valley began as a single classroom with 40 students in 1998. Today it's a full Nursery-to-XII campus — but the reason we started hasn't changed.

Our Story

How Greenwood Valley grew

A short history of the milestones that shaped the school you see today.

1998

Founded on Ridgeline Road

Greenwood Valley opened with two classrooms, 40 students, and a founding promise: every child deserves individual attention.

2004

CBSE affiliation secured

The school received formal CBSE affiliation, opening the primary wing to a structured national curriculum.

2011

Senior secondary wing added

Science, Commerce and Humanities streams launched for Classes XI–XII, alongside a new library and science block.

2018

New sports & arts campus

A dedicated sports ground, indoor courts, and a performing arts studio opened to support co-curricular growth.

2026

1,240+ students, one community

Today Greenwood Valley serves families across the city, with 68+ faculty members and a 97% Class X/XII pass rate.

What Guides Us

Our vision & mission

Our Vision

To be a school where academic excellence and strong character are treated as equally important — so every student leaves prepared not just for exams, but for life.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, disciplined and stimulating environment where every child is known personally, challenged academically, and encouraged to pursue their own strengths.

From Our Leadership

Founder, Manager & Principal's message

VO Mr. Vikram Oberoi Founder & Chairman, Greenwood Valley School

In 1998, I started Greenwood Valley with two classrooms and a simple belief — that a school should feel like an extension of home, not an institution. Every wall we've built since, every teacher we've hired, has been in service of that first idea. Nearly three decades later, that belief hasn't changed; only the number of children it reaches has.

RS Dr. Radhika Sharma Principal, Greenwood Valley School

When parents ask me what makes Greenwood Valley different, I tell them it's not one thing — it's the sum of small choices we make every day. We keep our classrooms small enough that no child is invisible. We hire teachers who stay long enough to watch a student grow. And we measure success not only in marksheets, but in the kind of person a child becomes by the time they leave us. That is the promise I renew every single year.

AK Mr. Anil Kapoor School Manager, Greenwood Valley School

A school is a promise made to a community, and we take that seriously — in the classrooms we build, the teachers we choose, and the standards we hold ourselves to, every single year. My role is to make sure that promise is backed by the right infrastructure, the right people, and the right values, so our Principal and teachers can focus on what matters most: the students.
